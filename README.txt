TWENTY-ONE — Birthday Website

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

CUSTOMIZE
- Replace the text inside the letter in index.html, or use the on-page letter editor.
- The first uploaded photo is used as the full-screen hero background.
- The second uploaded photo is used in the birthday greeting section.
- Add more pictures with the “Add photos” button. Those extra photos are shown in the open page session.
- Change colors, spacing, and typography in style.css.

NOTES
- The envelope opens the letter section.
- The music button plays a gentle original synthesized tone; it does not use a copyrighted song.
- Google Fonts are loaded online. The site still works offline with fallback fonts.
